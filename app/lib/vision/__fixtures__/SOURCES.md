# ResNet-50 integration fixtures

These eight images are used only for local integration tests. They were resized
to fit within 768 pixels and encoded as JPEG at 80-85% quality. Follow each link
for its author and license metadata.

- `dog.jpg`: [Golden Retriever close up.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_Retriever_close_up.jpg)
- `cat.jpg`: [Felis catus.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Felis%20catus.jpg)
- `bee.jpg`: [Apis mellifera Western honey bee.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Apis%20mellifera%20Western%20honey%20bee.jpg)
- `chicken.jpg`: [Hen chicken.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hen_chicken.jpg)
- `butterfly.jpg`: [A monarch butterfly.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_monarch_butterfly.jpg)
- `dragonfly.jpg`: [Dragonfly close up macro photo.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dragonfly_close_up_macro_photo.jpg)
- `frog.jpg`: [Green treefrog.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Green_treefrog.jpg)
- `ant.jpg`: [Maricopa harvester ant](https://www.flickr.com/photos/131104726@N02/31538269553/) (public domain, Insects Unlocked)

The fixtures are deliberately representative examples, not a claim that
ResNet-50 will recognize every photo of these broad animal categories.
